    What repairs cost

    Each bar is the total range, labour plus parts. The tag says where the labour price comes from. Anything marked estimate is ours.

    Is it worth repairing?

    Student guides list second-hand bikes in Groningen at roughly €70 to €200. FietsCheck compares the repair with what your bike is worth now: up to 40% repair it, 40 to 70% get a second quote, over 70% think about replacing it. This is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee.

    Sources: NS Fiets & Service (read 2 Oct 2026), Radar price guide (Aug 2021), Bikes Groningen, Domakin.

    About

    Built for Groningen cyclists

    FietsCheck was born in Groningen, the city of bikes. It helps riders understand a bike problem before they walk into a shop. It gives guidance, not a mechanic's inspection.

    Built by Zeeshan, a student who rides in Groningen every day. Every price and policy links to its source, and we earn nothing from the shops or brands we mention. Found a mistake? Tell us.
