    What repairs cost

    Each bar is the total range, labour plus parts. The tag says where the labour price comes from. Anything marked estimate is ours.

    Is it worth repairing?

    Student guides list second-hand bikes in Groningen at roughly €70 to €200. FietsCheck compares the repair with what your bike is worth now: up to 40% repair it, 40 to 70% get a second quote, over 70% think about replacing it. This is a rule of thumb that our survey will test.

    Sources: NS Fiets & Service (read 2 Oct 2026), Radar price guide (Aug 2021), Bikes Groningen, Domakin.
