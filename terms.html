Most important: FietsCheck gives guidance, not a mechanic's inspection. If your brakes, steering or frame feel wrong, do not ride. Walk the bike to a repair shop.

What FietsCheck is

FietsCheck suggests the likely cause of a bike problem, a safety rating, a typical repair price and a note in Dutch for the mechanic. It is free to use and in beta.

No guarantee

  • A diagnosis is a likely cause, based on what you tap and type. It can be wrong, and it cannot see hidden damage.
  • Prices are typical ranges, not quotes. Labour prices come from NS Fiets & Service and Radar, checked on 2 October 2026. Part prices are our estimates. Always ask the shop for a price before work starts.
  • DIY steps are general advice. Only do a repair if you feel safe doing it, with the right tools.
