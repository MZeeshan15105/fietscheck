FietsCheck

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: FietsCheck has no accounts, no tracking and no advertising. What you type stays on your own device.

Who we are

FietsCheck is made by Muhammad Zeeshan in Groningen, the Netherlands. For any privacy question, contact us on github.com/MZeeshan15105/fietscheck.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not ask for your name, email, location or phone number, and we do not use cookies, analytics or ad trackers.

What stays on your device

  • Bikes and checks you save in the Garage.
  • Small settings, such as whether you saw the welcome screens and which bike type you picked.

This data is kept in your browser's or app's local storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can delete it in the Garage with "Delete all my garage data", or by clearing your browser or app data.

"Was this diagnosis right?"

If you answer this question under a result with Yes or No, the app sends two things to our Google Form:

  • the fault the app showed, as a short code (for example brake_pads)
  • whether you said it was right or wrong

Nothing else is sent: no name, no email, no text you typed, no photo, no location and no bike details. "Not sure yet" sends nothing, and only your first answer for a result is sent. We cannot link an answer to you. Google, which hosts the form, receives the request like any website and may log technical data such as your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Services you may open from the app

Some buttons open other services. They have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps, when you tap "Open Maps" or a shop.
  • Google Forms, when you choose to fill in our anonymous survey. It collects no names or emails.
  • Online stores, when you tap a link to buy a part.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service safe. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (AVG) you may ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we hold no personal data, there is usually nothing to send. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

FietsCheck is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
